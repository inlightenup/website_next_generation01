01 · Technology · United States

ImpactIQ

Making impact as measurable, and as investable, as financial return.

Capital is moving toward impact faster than anyone can prove it works. ImpactIQ closes that gap. It is inlightenup’s proprietary platform for measuring and independently verifying what a project actually achieves, so funders commit on evidence they can diligence. As verified impact becomes the standard, ImpactIQ is the infrastructure built to set it.

Focus
Impact measurement and independent outcome verification
Where it stands
Active product, being structured as its own entity under K2 Capital
Role in the portfolio
The governance layer that verifies outcomes across other projects

02 · Civic technology · United States

GovIQ

Turning the machinery of government into something everyone can read.

Laws and policies shape every market and every community, yet most people cannot follow them. GovIQ changes that. Positioned as an AI-powered Bloomberg Terminal for laws and policies, it turns live legislative data into civic learning and better government service. The goal: a public that understands its government, and institutions that can act on what is coming.

Focus
Civic learning, policy intelligence and government service
Where it stands
Working MVP with live Federal Register and Congress.gov integration
inlightenup’s role
Originator and builder, from strategy to product

03 · Youth programming · Brazil

Planting Seeds

Changing what a second chance looks like for a generation of young people.

Every youth justice system faces the same question: how do young people leave better than they arrived? Planting Seeds, a program of Instituto Meditativos, answers it with mindfulness inside socio-education centers near Brasília, reaching about 2,500 young people across roughly 700 sessions. Centers across Brazil are asking for it, and the model is built to scale one facilitator and one center at a time.

Figures from the Planting Seeds campaign. Read the case study

Focus
Mindfulness-based facilitation for youth in state custody
Where it stands
First cohort closed. $60K places one trained facilitator in a new center for a year
inlightenup’s role
Organizational profile, public campaign and institutional positioning

04 · Fair-trade supply chains · Brazil

AMA Trade

Proving the forest is worth more standing than cleared.

Global demand for clean, traceable, natural ingredients keeps rising, and Brazil’s biomes are among the most biodiverse places on earth. AMA Trade connects the two. It sources premium Amazonian and Cerrado ingredients such as açaí, copaíba and buriti oil from Indigenous and traditional communities on fair-trade terms, and brings them to world markets as biocosmetics and commodities, including its own AMA Apothecary line.

Focus
Biocosmetics and traceable forest commodities
Where it stands
Pitch deck, valuation model and competitive analysis built
inlightenup’s role
Owner and commercial architect; university partnerships in development

05 · Workforce development · United States

North Beacon

Building the workforce an AI-shaped economy will actually need.

As AI reshapes professional work, the skills that matter most are the human ones: judgment, emotional intelligence and purpose. North Beacon develops them. College students work on live projects with real impact instead of case interviews, guided by mentors from banking and entrepreneurship across three labs: Emotional Intelligence, Critical Thinking and Purpose. Partner institutions meet talent that has already proven itself.

Focus
Talent development through live advisory projects
Where it stands
Program designed. 11 interns to date, 2 converted to active members
inlightenup’s role
Program owner, delivered with Head Start Career and university partners

06 · Conservation · Brazil

Terra of Tomorrow

A new model for keeping the world’s forests standing.

Forests survive when they are worth more alive than cut down. Terra of Tomorrow (Terra do Amanhã) is building that economy in Brazil’s Cerrado, starting in the Jalapão region of Tocantins. It pairs conservation with a regenerative bioeconomy so the people who live in the forest prosper by protecting it. It is designed as a model that can travel, with expansion to the United States already planned.

Focus
Forest preservation and regenerative bioeconomy
Where it stands
Structured in Brazil as Instituto Terra do Amanhã; US expansion planned
inlightenup’s role
Strategic review, funder materials and university partnerships
